## Summary
Moritz "Moe" Wagner is a 29-year-old center who signed a 2-year, $19 million deal with the Brooklyn Nets on July 1, 2026, completing the Nets' center rotation. Standing 6'11" and weighing 245 lbs, Wagner brings versatility and floor-spacing to complement the Nets' scoring-forward duo of [[Michael Porter Jr.]] ($38.3M) and [[Julius Randle]] ($30.9M). The signing reflects the organization's 2026-27 competitive pivot—rounding out a modern frontcourt capable of spacing the floor and sharing the ball.

## Key Profile
- **Position**: Center/Power Forward
- **Physical**: 6'11", 245 lbs, age 29
- **Contract Status**: 2-year, $19M deal (signed 7/1/26); $5.0M first-year salary per 2026-07-03 roster
- **Role**: Floor-spacing big, complementary center, interior depth

## Nets Context
Wagner arrives as the final piece in a restructured center rotation. With [[Nic Claxton]] traded to Chicago on draft day (June 23, 2026) for cap flexibility, the Nets needed depth behind the young, developing core of [[Danny Wolf]] (6'11", stretch big) and [[Day'Ron Sharpe]] (6'10", rim protector). Wagner fits the modern NBA mold: he can shoot from three, pass from the high post, and move defensively. His presence stabilizes the position while allowing Wolf and Sharpe to develop without carrying the full load.

Alongside [[Joshua Jefferson]] (Iowa State, #28 pick, 6'8", drafted 2026-06-23), Wagner completes a versatile frontcourt supporting the MPJ-Randle scoring pair and young point guards ([[Egor Demin]], [[Mikel Brown Jr.]], [[Nolan Traore]]).

## Playing Style
Wagner is the cerebral big man. At 6'11", he can:
- Stretch the floor with reliable three-point shooting
- Find cutters from the high post (high basketball IQ)
- Switch onto smaller players and hold his own defensively
- Rebound and move on the glass

He's not an elite shot-blocker or rim runner, but his value is secondary scoring and floor balance — exactly what a team with MPJ, Randle, and young playmakers needs.
