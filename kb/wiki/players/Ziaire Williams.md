## Summary
Ziaire Williams, a 6'9" forward, had his $6.25M team option declined by the Brooklyn Nets on June 29, 2026, making him an unrestricted free agent. Despite posting a career year in 2025-26, the Nets chose not to retain Williams, signaling a shift in roster philosophy toward the acquisition of [[Julius Randle]] and younger drafted prospects.

## Nets Context
Williams' declined option reflects the organizational pivot toward veteran scorers and youth development. The 2026 draft acquisition of [[Julius Randle]] ($30.9M) and drafted forwards ([[Joshua Jefferson]], [[Tyler Bilodeau]]) created a crowded forward rotation, reducing Williams' rotation opportunities and financial priority relative to the Nets' rebuild architecture. His role was secondary to [[Michael Porter Jr.]] ($38.3M) and would have been further diminished by Randle's arrival.
