## Summary

Standing 6'4" and weighing 175 lbs, Ellis brings defensive perimeter pressure and backcourt stability to complement [[Egor Demin]] and the Nets' young point guard development. The signing reflects the organization's 2026-27 competitive pivot—after a 20-win tank season, the Nets are prioritizing defensive support and veteran guidance for their rookie-heavy roster.
